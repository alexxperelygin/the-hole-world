Privacy Policy

The Hole World · Draft v0.1 · Last updated: 2026-11-02

Placeholder for Phase 1. The final text is reviewed by the operator's legal counsel before launch and before Paddle domain approval. Not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Everyone: an anonymous random device identifier, your chosen country, game progress (depth, coins, tools, finds) and coarse technical data needed to run the service (IP-derived country, user agent). No name, email, phone or precise location. No advertising identifiers, no third-party ad or analytics trackers; usage statistics, if any, are cookieless and aggregated.
  • Subscribers: the email address and payment status provided by the payment processor (Paddle, NOWPayments or Telegram), used only to grant and restore the subscription. Card details never reach us.
  • Telegram Mini App: your Telegram user ID, used to link your progress and subscription.

Why

To run the game, show the world ranking, protect it from cheating, deliver what you paid for, and comply with the law. Legal bases where GDPR applies: performance of a contract and legitimate interest (fraud prevention); consent for optional notifications.

Children

The Game is designed for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; non-paying players are anonymous. Purchases are made by the cardholder.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure. Processors: Cloudflare (hosting), Paddle, NOWPayments, Telegram (payments). We do not sell data.

Your rights

You can erase local progress in Settings → Erase progress. To access, export or delete server-side data, contact support@thehole.world with your device identifier (shown in Settings). Retention: saves are deleted after 12 months of inactivity; payment records are kept as long as the law requires.

Cookies

Only local storage for your save and preferences. No tracking cookies, so no cookie banner.
