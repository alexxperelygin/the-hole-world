Refund Policy

Placeholder for Phase 1. The final text is reviewed by the operator's legal counsel before launch and before Paddle domain approval. Not legal advice.

Golden Shovel (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay)

Purchases are processed by Paddle as Merchant of Record and are subject to Paddle's buyer terms. You can request a full refund within 14 days of a first purchase or renewal if you have not used the perks meaningfully, and in any case where the law of your country requires it. Contact support@thehole.world or Paddle support with your order number. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep the perks until the end of the paid period.

Crypto passes (NOWPayments)

30/90/365-day passes are one-time, non-recurring purchases. Because crypto transfers are irreversible, refunds are issued as a credit (extension of the pass) or, where required by law, in the original currency to the sending wallet, minus network fees.

Telegram Stars

Subscriptions paid with Stars are managed inside Telegram. Refunds and cancellations follow Telegram's Stars terms; we process them through the Bot API when eligible.

Accidental and underage purchases

If a child purchased without permission, contact us within 30 days and we will refund and cancel the subscription.

How long it takes

We reply within 3 business days. Card refunds appear within 5–10 business days depending on the bank.
